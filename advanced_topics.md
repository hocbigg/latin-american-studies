# Advanced Topics

After completing the Core Undergraduate Curriculum, learners should select **2 to 4 specialization tracks** based on their research interests, regional focus, or professional trajectory. These tracks build upon foundational knowledge to engage with graduate-level monographs, theoretical frameworks, primary archives, and contemporary policy debates.

### Specialization Tracks Index

- **[Decoloniality, Indigenous Autonomy, and Afro-Latin Politics](#decoloniality-indigenous-autonomy-and-afro-latin-politics):** Investigates the coloniality of power, Indigenous self-determination, plurinational statehood, and Afro-descendant territorial movements.
- **[Political Economy, Neo-Extractivism, and Environmental Justice](#political-economy-neo-extractivism-and-environmental-justice):** Examines resource-driven development models, agrarian reform, commodity frontiers, and grassroots ecological defense movements.
- **[State Violence, Authoritarianism, and Transitional Memory](#state-violence-authoritarianism-and-transitional-memory):** Analyzes Cold War counterinsurgency, revolutionary guerrilla struggles, human rights archives, and the politics of memorialization.
- **[Hemispheric Geopolitics, Borders, and Transnational Migration](#hemispheric-geopolitics-borders-and-transnational-migration):** Explores asymmetrical inter-American relations, border regimes, transit corridors, diaspora networks, and illicit transnational economies.
- **[Gender, Feminist Political Ecology, and Queer Mobilizations](#gender-feminist-political-ecology-and-queer-mobilizations):** Assesses decolonial feminisms, anti-femicide legal mobilization, territorial care defense, and LGBTQ+ citizenship struggles.
